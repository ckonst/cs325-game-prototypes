Biggie Cheese Challenge

Play as Biggie Cheese and shoot your enemies out of your minecraft base in this action-packed platforming-shooter challenge.

  • Gameplay:

    • Movement: WASD or Arrow keys

    Shooting: F

    Jump: Spacebar

    Menu Controls: Mouse Movement and Mouse Button 1 to select Menu Items.

  • Play Time: 1-2 minutes.
